How it plays

Houston plans your day to the five minutes. You work the steps, keep the air clean, exercise twice, and handle what breaks.

The plan

Every block has a start time.

Wake, breakfast, science, exercise, maintenance. Tap Begin, work the steps, and watch the clock. Late adds up.

Off-nominal

Things break. You choose what it costs.

The CO2 scrubber stops. Fix it yourself, start the backup, or let the ground work it while the air gets worse. The clock holds while you decide.

The Cupola

Look down when you get the chance.

Earth-photo passes put a 400 mm lens in your hands. Frame the Richat Structure or the Bahama Bank before the station carries you past.

Daily summary

Houston grades the day.

On-time rate, science, workouts, peak CO2, sleep. Then lights out, and you wake into the next one.
